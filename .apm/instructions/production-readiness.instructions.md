### Production readiness

Build every feature to production-ready standards from its first implementation.

Do not use labels such as MVP, prototype, test release, or initial version to justify deferring essential engineering work. Every delivered capability must include an appropriate level of:

- correctness;
- reliability;
- security;
- maintainability;
- observability;
- test coverage;
- failure handling.

When time or resources constrain delivery, reduce the number of capabilities in scope. Do not lower the engineering quality of the capabilities that remain.
